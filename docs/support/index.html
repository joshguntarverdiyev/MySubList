Frequently asked questions

How do I add, edit, or delete a subscription?

Tap the + button on the Home screen to add one. To edit or delete, open the subscription and use the options on its details screen.

How does the AI Advisor work and are there limits?

The AI Advisor reads your subscriptions and suggests ways to save. Free users get 5 messages per day; Premium users get 50 messages per day.

What is the difference between Free and Premium?

Free includes up to 5 subscriptions and 5 AI messages per day. Premium unlocks unlimited subscriptions and 50 AI messages per day for €3.99/mo or €29.99/yr.

How does currency and FX conversion work?

Pick your preferred currency in Profile settings. Subscriptions in other currencies are converted using recent exchange rates so your totals stay consistent.

How are calendar and renewal dates calculated?

We calculate the next renewal from each subscription’s start date and billing cycle. The Calendar tab shows upcoming renewals with color-coded dots.

Will I get notified before a renewal?

Yes. Enable notifications and set your reminder lead time in Profile settings to get a local alert before each renewal.

How is my data kept private and secure?

Your data is stored securely via Supabase and never sold to third parties. Auth tokens are kept in your device’s secure storage.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile, open your account, and use the delete option in the Danger Zone. This permanently removes your account and all associated data.

How do billing and payments work?

Premium is billed through the App Store or Google Play. Manage or cancel your plan anytime from your store account settings.

How do I contact support?

Email support@mysublist.app for help, or use the contact addresses above for general and business inquiries. We aim to reply within a couple of days.
