# Privacy Policy

**App:** MySubList
**Effective date:** July 20, 2026
**Contact:** hello@mysublist.app

MySubList ("we", "us", "our") helps you track your digital subscriptions, see upcoming renewals, and get AI-powered saving advice. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use and store it, and the rights you have over it. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

## 1. Data we collect

We only collect the data needed to run the app:

- **Account data** — your email address, and (optionally) your name and profile photo, used to create and secure your account.
- **Subscription data** — the subscriptions you add: name, plan, price, currency, billing period, dates, payment method label, notes, and trial details.
- **AI Advisor messages** — the questions you send to the AI Advisor and the replies you receive.
- **App preferences** — settings such as currency, language, first day of the week, and notification timing.

We do **not** collect your payment card numbers or connect to your real bank or financial accounts. Subscription entries are labels you type in yourself.

## 2. How we use your data

We use your data only to:

- provide and secure your account and the core app features;
- calculate spending totals and schedule local renewal reminders on your device;
- generate personalized saving advice through the AI Advisor.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, and we do **not** sell it.

## 3. How we store and protect your data

Your data is stored securely using **Supabase** (PostgreSQL), hosted on EU servers. Access is protected by row-level security so that each user can only access their own data. Authentication tokens on your device are kept in the operating system's secure storage (Keychain / Keystore), never in plain storage.

## 4. Who we share your data with

We do **not** sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

The only external processing happens when you use the **AI Advisor**: the relevant subscription details and your message are sent to **Google Gemini** through our secure server-side function to generate a reply. Google processes this data only to return the response and does not receive your account credentials. If you never use the AI Advisor, none of your data is sent to Gemini.

We may disclose data if strictly required by law.

## 5. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU (including Italy), you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data (editable in-app).
- **Erasure** — delete your account and all associated data.
- **Portability / Export** — receive your data in a portable format.
- **Objection / Restriction** — object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email **hello@mysublist.app**. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Italy, the *Garante per la protezione dei dati personali*).

## 6. How to delete your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from **Profile & Settings → Delete Account**. This permanently removes your profile, all subscriptions, and all AI messages from our database (cascade delete). Alternatively, email **hello@mysublist.app** and we will delete your data on request.

## 7. Data retention

We keep your data only while your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our production systems promptly. Residual copies in encrypted backups are cycled out on our normal backup rotation.

## 8. Children

MySubList is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above reflects the latest version. Material changes will be communicated in-app or by email.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at **hello@mysublist.app**.
